Project: Factory of the Future

Description: A leading aerospace manufacturer struggled with legacy systems, manual tracking, fragmented data, and hidden bottlenecks on its factory floor.

Highlights:

  • HCLTech created a live digital twin of the production line, using sensors and real-time data to mirror operations virtually.
  • This implementation enables the manufacturer to see problems faster and optimize workflows.
  • AI agents are fundamentally changing engineering operations by managing inspection, reasoning, and reporting.
  • Impact: The solution significantly improves safety, turnaround time, and reliability while cutting costs.
